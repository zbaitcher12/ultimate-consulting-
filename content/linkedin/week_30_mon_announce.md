A community college I worked with had commissioned three documentation projects in four years. All three had produced a document. None of the three had produced operational change.

The failure looked like a delivery problem. It wasn't. The delivery had been fine each time. The failure was in the decisions made before kickoff.

Scope. All three projects had been scoped to a function rather than a workflow. That produces documents too general to use. Sponsor. All three had presidential sponsorship without a named executive owner. That produces work with no accountable center. Sequencing. All three had started with the workflow that was easiest to document rather than the one that mattered most. Governance. None had named the internal owner before the write-up landed. Post-project ownership. None had a refresh cadence.

Five decisions predict the outcome. All five had gone the wrong way three times running.
