A community college I worked with was six months into SACSCOC self-study when we met. The team had built a parallel evidence-assembly workstream. Every operational department was being asked to contribute artifacts on request.

Halfway through, someone in the accreditation office ran the exercise the institution should have run in year one. She mapped SACSCOC evidence requirements against operational documentation the institution already had.

Most of the accreditation package was work the institution was already doing. The financial-aid procedures satisfied the financial-responsibility standards. The student-learning assessment workflows satisfied the assessment standards. The IT change-management runbooks satisfied the resource-integrity standards.

The accreditation team and the operating-model team had never had the conversation that connected the two. They'd run as parallel workstreams for years.

Doubling the work isn't a strategy. Recognizing where the work is already done is.
