Compliance isn't the same as audit-ready. Compliance is doing things correctly. Audit-ready is being able to show you did.

Most institutions are compliant. Far fewer are audit-ready. The gap between the two is where findings live.

FVT/GE reporting deadline is October 1. Over 1,900 institutions were publicly named as under-reporters last cycle. If your first knowledge of a documentation gap comes from a federal review, you've already paid for it — you just haven't been billed yet.
