Process owner works when the function has a stable supervisor with bandwidth to own the refresh. Governance committee works when your institution has cross-functional alignment but inconsistent functional tenure. Embedded ops works only at institutions with the staffing depth to dedicate one to three positions.

At concentrated-staffing institutions, the first two are the fit. The decision is institution-specific — and it has to be made before the work closes, not after.

Which of the three models fits your institution's staffing — and which one is your last project actually running on?
