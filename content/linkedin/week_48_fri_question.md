The three structural differences are visible in the engagement letter. Scope on short cycles per piece of work, not six-month projects. Handoff to internal ownership as the closing document, not a downstream stakeholder responsibility. Economic structure that fits repeated pieces across the portfolio, not sustained retainer on one function.

The pattern is legible before signing. The RFP either invites it or excludes it.

What would your next outside piece of work look like if capability transfer were the primary scoping criterion — and how different would that be from your last one?
