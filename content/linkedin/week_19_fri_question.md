In event mode, the program review is an interruption. In operating mode, it's a verification.

The structural difference is not effort. It's cadence. The institution running operating mode is doing roughly the same total work — just distributed across the year, folded into how each function runs, and retrievable when the reviewer asks.

The institutions that make the shift stop losing quarters to review prep.

What would your operations look like next quarter if this quarter's program review took three days instead of ten weeks?
