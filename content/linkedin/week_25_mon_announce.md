A public regional university I worked with had three cross-functional handoffs that failed reliably every term. Aid-to-bursar timing on disbursement posting. Registrar-to-IT propagation on withdrawal cases. Advancement-to-finance recording on restricted gifts.

Each handoff had documentation. In every case, the documentation described the happy path. The workflow that mattered was the exception path, and the exception path wasn't in any document.

The aid office had a workflow for the standard disbursement. It didn't have a workflow for the case where the disbursement posted before the enrollment count locked. The registrar had a policy for a standard withdrawal. It didn't have a policy for the mid-term withdrawal that crossed a Title IV boundary.

Within-department SOPs have a clear owner. Cross-functional handoffs don't. That's where the actual work lives. That's also where the SOP work almost never gets scoped.
