Pairs with blog post: *The 60-Second Decision Test*
Format: ~95 words. Direct engagement bait.

Sixty seconds is not arbitrary. It's the threshold above which staff who need a decision will start to route around the chain and figure it out on their own.

Below sixty seconds, the chain works. Above sixty seconds, the chain is decoration, and the actual routing is being reconstructed informally every time an exception hits.

The staff routing informally are not the problem. The chain that forces them to route informally is.

If a new employee had to route the three most common exceptions on your campus this week, would they name the correct owner in sixty seconds — without asking?
