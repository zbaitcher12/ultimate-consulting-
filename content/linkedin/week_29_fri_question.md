The three categories of finding surface differently at fast cadence. Workflow gaps become SOP revisions. Authority gaps become RACI extensions. Measurement gaps become metric instrumentation work.

At slow cadence, the same three categories surface as a list — and the list becomes the finished document, without ever mapping to a decision.

The categories don't change. The cycle time changes what your institution does with them.

If your last gap analysis produced a list rather than a decision, what would have happened differently if the finding had landed in two weeks instead of six months?
