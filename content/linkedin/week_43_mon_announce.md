A community college CFO I worked with had a cash-application aging report he ran every month. Aged buckets. Trend lines. The number he'd been watching for six years.

He'd never connected the report to a bursar workflow. The aging wasn't primarily a payment-source issue. It was primarily a bursar exception-handling issue. Every day of aging that sat above his expected threshold was downstream of a documentation gap in the exception-handling workflow.

The finding-to-close cycle he tracked for audit remediation had the same relationship to documentation refresh capacity. His exception-rate trends had the same relationship to underlying documentation across a half-dozen functions.

The metrics weren't wrong. They were treated as financial metrics when their actual causal chain ran through the record of how the work runs. He'd been managing three symptoms that shared the same underlying variable.

Once he saw the connection, the priorities on the CFO desk reorganized.
