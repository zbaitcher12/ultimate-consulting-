The SOPs that close marketing gaps aren't lengthy. The campaign brief SOP is a template plus a routing for completion. The approval chain SOP is a one-page workflow that names the reviewers and the timing. The asset library governance SOP is the access policy plus a refresh cadence.

None of the three constrain creative work. All three protect the brand equity your institution has already paid for.

If your CMO left this quarter, which of the three would you rather already have in writing — and why isn't it?
