Format: ~90 words. Direct engagement bait.

The briefing your incoming CIO should be handed on day one is not a document that gets written on day one. It gets written six months before the transition — by the people who still remember why the decisions were made, while the context is still live.

Most institutions write it after the transition, when the new CIO is already making decisions in the dark. That's the pattern the war story comes from.

If your CIO gave notice this quarter, would the briefing already exist — or would you start building it in week two?
