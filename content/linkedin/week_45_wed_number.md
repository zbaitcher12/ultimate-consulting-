Three numbers to put against the equity-outcomes plan.

The cycle-time differential in financial aid verification at institutions whose workflow wasn't on paper against non-standard document formats: 2 to 5 additional weeks for students whose situations don't map cleanly — concentrated on first-in-family college students, returning adult learners, and families your institution most publicly commits to serving.

The annual retention loss when case-management workflows produce consistent guidance for standard cases and improvised guidance for non-standard cases: 40 to 120 students per term at a mid-size institution, disproportionately from the populations the strategic plan named as priorities.

The institutional cost of new programmatic interventions that were funded to address outcome gaps a documentation refresh would have closed at a fraction of the cost: $250K–$900K per program, per year, running for 3 to 5 years before the pattern gets recognized.

None of these are theoretical. I've watched all three at real institutions.

The investment required to put the three highest-impact workflows on paper against the full range of student situations — including the non-standard ones: a fraction of any one of those numbers.
