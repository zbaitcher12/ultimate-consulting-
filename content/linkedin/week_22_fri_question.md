A concentrated IR function is not a failure of design. It's a consequence of staffing calibrated for throughput. Most institutions run one-deep IR for a reason.

What can be built is documentation that makes the one-deep function survivable. Data definitions in writing. Refresh cadence on a calendar. Dashboard governance in a workflow. FERPA boundaries in a routing rule.

The SOPs are lightweight. What they protect isn't.

If your IR director gave notice next month, which of the four gaps would leave you most exposed — and how quickly could you close it before their departure?
