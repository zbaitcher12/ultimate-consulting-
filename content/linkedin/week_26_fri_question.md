The uncomfortable observation from twelve weeks of functional deep-dives: institution-wide maturity is below the average of its functional maturities.

The cross-functional handoffs are where the drag lives. An institution with three high-maturity functions and three low-maturity functions doesn't operate at average maturity. It operates at the maturity of the lowest function in any given cross-functional workflow.

Which of your functional areas is the drag on the handoffs it participates in — and what would raising it half a level do to your cross-functional risk?
