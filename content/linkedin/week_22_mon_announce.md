A mid-size private institution I worked with had one institutional-research director. She handled IPEDS, accreditation evidence, board dashboards, strategic-plan metrics, and every ad hoc cabinet request that needed a number by Thursday.

When she went on medical leave in October, the institution discovered how much of the operating model had been sitting in her memory. The data definitions. The refresh cadence. The dashboard logic. The FERPA boundaries. The list of which cabinet members preferred which cut of the enrollment data.

The interim staff member wasn't underqualified. She was under-briefed by an institution that had never written down what only one person knew.

Your IR director is concentrated by design. Four documentation gaps produce most of the transition risk, and none require additional headcount to close. They just require somebody to write them down before the person who carries them leaves.
