Pairs with blog post: *The Gap Analysis That Changes the Conversation*
Format: ~90 words. Direct engagement bait.

---

Generic gap analysis names everything in general and nothing in particular. It's the finished document that surfaces what leadership already suspected, at a scope your institution can't act on, in a timeline that arrives after the cabinet has moved to the next priority.

The version that produces decisions is narrower, faster, and more specific — one workflow, three findings, one action matrix.

If you ran a two-week gap analysis on your single highest-Title IV-volume workflow next month, what would it surface — and what decision would it force?
