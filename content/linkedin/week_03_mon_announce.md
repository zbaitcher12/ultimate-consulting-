A mid-size private institution I sat with in the fall had a cabinet that met every Thursday to resolve cross-functional exceptions. Aid-plus-IT questions. Bursar-plus-registrar timing issues. A residency-classification appeal that had been sitting for two weeks.

Somewhere in the second hour, the provost asked why the residency appeal was on the cabinet agenda. Nobody answered. Then a dean said the registrar had asked general counsel, general counsel had asked the CFO, and the CFO had asked to put it on the cabinet.

The RACI existed. It named an owner for exactly that scenario. Three of the five people in the escalation chain had never read it.

The cabinet was absorbing four hours a week of routing work because the documented chain lived in a slide deck nobody had opened since the last reorganization. The chart existed. The chain didn't.
