**Week 1 · Friday Question Post · LinkedIn**
Pairs with blog post: *Why Your ERP Goes Live—But Your Institution Doesn't*
Format: ~80 words. Direct engagement bait. One question.

---

Institutions that go live "on time" often report the highest post-go-live cleanup costs. The math isn't intuitive.

Institutional readiness isn't measured by the project timeline. It's measured by whether the workflows the system was configured around are the workflows your staff actually follow — on paper, consistently, with a trail that holds up when someone asks "says who?"

If your ERP went live tomorrow, how many of your critical cross-functional workflows would fail a five-minute paper test?
