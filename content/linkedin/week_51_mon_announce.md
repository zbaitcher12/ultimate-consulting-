A regional state system I worked with had consumed every recent year-end window with accounting close, holiday planning, and the cabinet retreat that produced the next year's priorities. The documentation portfolio had drifted through every one of those years without a refresh cycle.

The window between December and January is the right time for portfolio-level work. Fall term is closed. Spring hasn't started. The audit cycle for the prior fiscal year has reached the field-work phase. Your institution has visibility into what needs attention.

We built a five-component cadence for the next year-end. Portfolio inventory. Lightweight refresh on current-under-cadence documents. Full rewrite on documents change events had rendered stale. Governance re-check on ownership assignments. Refresh-cadence calibration for the coming year.

The cadence took roughly thirty hours of coordinated staff time across the whole system. That's less than any single scramble the year had already absorbed.
