Decision architecture isn't an org chart. Org charts show who reports to whom. Decision architecture shows who decides what — when the situation is one the org chart doesn't cover.

Most institutions confuse the two. That's why cross-functional exceptions escalate.

If you handed a new employee a list of the ten most common cross-departmental exceptions on your campus, could they name the correct decision owner for each — without asking?
