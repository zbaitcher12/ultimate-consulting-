The documented handoff usually describes a clean version in the happy path. The actual handoff includes the timing mismatches, the manual interventions, and the workarounds staff invented to keep the workflow moving.

Both are true. Only one is written down. When the audit hits, only one is defensible.

The joint-ownership SOP is the fix — same document, same routing, both departments named as co-owners.

Which of your cross-functional handoffs would fail the audit-sample test — and which of the two departments would each blame the other?
