**Week 2 · Friday Question Post · LinkedIn**
Pairs with blog post: *The SOP Nobody Wrote Is the Risk Nobody Sees*
Format: ~80 words. Direct engagement bait.

Institutional knowledge doesn't disappear on the day someone leaves. It disappears on the day something changes — a system, a workflow, a regulation — and no one on the current team was around to remember why the workaround exists.

The person who carries the workflow isn't the risk. The absence of a written record is.

If the person who runs your most complex cross-functional workflow walked out tomorrow, how many weeks until the operation showed signs of it?
