A public regional university I worked with last quarter finished ERP cutover on schedule. Clean go-live. Green status report. Cabinet celebrated at the standing Thursday meeting.

Three weeks in, a financial aid specialist was still running the old spreadsheet on the side. The new workflow had been configured around a process that had only ever lived in her head. Not sabotage. Not resistance. Just the only way she knew to get aid packaged on time for the students counting on it that week.

The finance team caught it in a routine reconciliation. The IT team documented the workaround after the fact. And the cabinet slowly realized the system had gone live, but the institution hadn't. Staff had learned the new interface while quietly preserving the old workflow underneath it.

The technical readiness gauges were all green. The question nobody had asked was whether the record of how work gets done had ever made it onto paper.
