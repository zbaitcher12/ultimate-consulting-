The three trigger types catch different drift. Calendar triggers catch the drift that happens quietly. Change triggers catch the drift that follows a system or staff transition. Audit triggers catch the drift that surfaces because someone external asked.

Institutions that use only one trigger miss the drift the other two would catch. Institutions that use all three catch drift consistently.

Which of the three triggers is running most reliably at your institution — and which one is your portfolio missing the most?
