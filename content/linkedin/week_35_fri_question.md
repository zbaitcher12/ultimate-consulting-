The institutions that have integrated report accreditation cycles consuming measurably less bandwidth, and stronger evidence packages — because the evidence comes from the way the work actually runs rather than from a parallel assembly.

One portfolio, two audiences. The accreditor's evidence request maps to the operational document. The operational document names the accreditor's standard.

If your accreditation liaison and your operating-model owner had a two-hour session next month, what percentage of your next reaffirmation evidence package could you map to documents that already exist — or should?
