The two-week cycle is more demanding on your institution than the multi-month cycle. Two hours with the staff who do the work. One hour with the supervisor. Thirty minutes with the audit liaison. All inside week one.

That's the entire institutional lift. What it produces is a finished document that lands while everyone still remembers the conversation.

If a first conversation happened at your institution next Monday, could the participants clear three and a half hours between them that week — and would the draft they'd see by Friday of week two be worth it?
