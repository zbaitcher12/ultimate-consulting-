Three structural commitments make the difference. A named owner with authority. A documented work plan with quarterly milestones. Dedicated documentation refresh capacity that doesn't compete with day-to-day operations.

None of them are heroic. All of them require a leadership decision to fund the twenty-four months after go-live as a distinct program — not as project overrun.

The institutions that fund the optimization window build the discipline. The ones that defer it discover, at month twenty-four, that the modernization landed the system but not the value.

Which of the three commitments does your post-modernization plan already include — and which is still missing?
