The window is short. Within ninety days, the new hire has absorbed the workarounds into normal practice and can no longer see the gaps as gaps. The learning window closes.

The institutions that structure onboarding around the two outputs — independent productivity and documentation improvement — capture the observation while it's still fresh.

If a new hire started at your institution next Monday, would orientation explicitly invite them to flag documentation gaps — or would the invitation be implicit and easy to miss?
