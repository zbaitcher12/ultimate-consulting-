A quarter is short. It's also long enough for the pattern to be visible.

The institutions that stalled in Q1 usually had at least one of three broken. They named the work generically and didn't act on it. They had presidential sponsorship without a defined executive owner. Or they committed to the work with no cadence, so it drifted into the open-ended-initiative category nobody is responsible for closing.

Which of the three patterns held at your institution through Q1 — and which one broke?
