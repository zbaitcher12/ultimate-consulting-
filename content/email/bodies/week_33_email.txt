Documentation does not refresh itself. The workflow the document describes keeps evolving — staff change, systems update, regulations shift, exceptions accumulate — and the document that was accurate at publication becomes inaccurate over time. The institutions that treat documents as one-time outputs produce documents that decay. The institutions that treat them as renewable assets produce documents that compound.

Three things the piece names:
- Trigger events that start a refresh.
- The distinction between lightweight refresh and full rewrite.
- Version control and change-log discipline.
