Major gifts officers are the function that resists SOPs most consistently in higher education. The resistance has a reason. Gift cultivation is judgment-intensive, relationship-based, and frequently bespoke to each donor. The major gifts officer who has cultivated a seven-figure donor over five years has accumulated a body of relationship knowledge that does not lend itself to a template.

Three things the piece names:
- Contact reporting.
- Portfolio assignment and reassignment.
- Annual wealth screening and pipeline refresh.
