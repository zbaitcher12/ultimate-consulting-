Federal program reviews and external audits in higher education typically arrive on a predictable cadence. The institutions that exit reviews cleanly are the institutions whose document portfolios have produced the evidence the reviewer is going to ask for, before the reviewer asks. The institutions that exit with findings are the institutions that assembled the evidence in event mode — the six-week push before the review opens.

---

## The pipeline is not a workstream.

The audit-readiness pipeline is the operating cadence that moves documents through five stages in continuous flow.

**Identification.** Naming which workflows require audit-ready documentation. The list is institution-specific.

**Documentation.** Producing the documents the identified workflows require. Development follows the cadence established elsewhere in the operating cycle.

**Evidence-mapping.** Connecting each document to the audit-evidence requirements it addresses. The R2T4 document maps to the R2T4 evidence requirements federal reviewers ask for.

**Peer review.** Subjecting each document to internal review by a staff member outside the originating function. The review checks whether the document would hold up for a reviewer who is not familiar with the workflow.

**Leadership sign-off.** Closing each document into your institution's audit-ready portfolio. The sign-off creates the accountability chain the pipeline depends on.

---

## Why the pipeline beats event-mode prep.

In event mode, the program review pulls operations into evidence assembly for six to twelve weeks, during which other work pauses. In operating mode — with the pipeline running — the program review is a verification of the work your institution was already doing, and operations does not pause.

---

## A question worth asking now.

Is your institution running an audit-readiness pipeline — or event-mode prep each time a review opens?

The pipeline is what makes the program review uneventful. The institutions that build it have fewer findings, less remediation cost, and more operational capacity available for the work your institution exists to do.
