The cycle time between the first conversation and the first audit-ready document is the single most important variable in whether documentation work actually lands. Cycle times that stretch past a month consistently produce documents that arrive in a different institutional context than the one in which they were commissioned. The cabinet that funded the work has moved on. The staff who participated in the first conversation have been pulled into the next crisis. The document lands in a context where the urgency has dissipated.

---

## The two-week cycle has a different structure.

The institutions that get value from documentation work run on a two-week first-conversation-to-document cycle. Capture happens in the first week. Draft delivery happens in the second. Refinement and final acceptance happen in week three.

Two weeks is not a compression of a six-week cycle into two weeks. It is a different structure.

The scope is narrow — one workflow, one functional area, one specific compliance gap. The participants are tightly defined. The write-up is calibrated to the two-week window.

---

## The first conversation is structured.

Two hours with the staff who do the work. One hour with the supervisor. Thirty minutes with the audit liaison.

The conversation surfaces the documented version, the actual version, the exceptions, the workarounds, and the cross-functional dependencies. The output is a structured capture record that feeds the drafting in week two.

---

## What the two-week cycle produces.

The drafting in week two produces the first audit-ready document. Narrow in scope but complete in detail. It captures the workflow at the level of operational reality. It names the exceptions and includes the workaround steps. It specifies the audit trail. It names the workflow owner with refresh authority.

Week three closes the loop. The draft circulates to the participants. Feedback is incorporated. The final document is published. The named owner takes possession.

---

## Why the cycle works.

Every step has a constrained scope and a constrained timeline. The capture does not expand into the entire function. The drafting does not expand into a comprehensive portfolio. The refinement does not expand into a multi-round review. The constraint produces the document inside the institutional context window.

---

## A question worth asking now.

What's the longest your institution has waited for a documentation draft after the first conversation — and what would change if it took two weeks?

Two weeks is the window inside which institutional context stays live. The cycles that fit inside the window produce documents your institution uses.
