Major gifts officers are the function that resists SOPs most consistently in higher education. The resistance has a reason. Gift cultivation is judgment-intensive, relationship-based, and frequently bespoke to each donor. The major gifts officer who has cultivated a seven-figure donor over five years has accumulated a body of relationship knowledge that does not lend itself to a template.

---

## The resistance carries load-bearing risk.

The donor pipeline that lives in the major gifts officer's head is the donor pipeline that disappears when the major gifts officer takes a position at another institution.

Advancement teams have all had this conversation. The departed staff member leaves with the relationships. The relationships do not transfer cleanly. Your institution rebuilds the pipeline from the next staff member's blank slate.

---

## The SOPs that solve this don't templatize the cultivation.

The SOPs document the institutional pipeline infrastructure that runs underneath the relationship work. Four advancement workflows show up consistently underdocumented.

**Contact reporting.** The standard for capturing donor conversations in a form that survives staff turnover.

**Portfolio assignment and reassignment.** The workflow for moving prospects between staff members without losing context.

**Annual wealth screening and pipeline refresh.** The cadence and process for updating the prospect pool.

**Stewardship cycle.** The routing for donor recognition, impact reporting, and ongoing relationship maintenance.

---

## Each one captured without constraining the cultivation.

The contact-report SOP specifies what to capture, not what to say. The portfolio reassignment SOP specifies the handoff briefing, not the cultivation strategy. The wealth-screening SOP specifies the data refresh cadence, not the prospect judgment. The stewardship SOP specifies the touchpoint timing, not the message.

---

## A question worth asking now.

How much of your donor pipeline knowledge would survive the departure of your three most senior gift officers?

Major gifts officers do not want SOPs for cultivation. They are right that cultivation does not template. They are wrong that nothing about the pipeline templates. The advancement leaders who get this distinction build pipelines that compound.
