Your bursar's exception queue is one of the most reliable diagnostics of how the work actually runs in your institution. Walk into any bursar's office and ask to see the queue. The size matters less than the composition. The composition tells you how much of the bursar's work is responding to predictable exceptions — exceptions that repeat every term — because the upstream workflows are not catching them.

---

## Six bursar workflows that show up undocumented.

Most institutions never write down the bursar workflows that produce the highest audit-finding risk.

**Cash application.** When a payment arrives without a clean match to a student account, how does staff resolve it.

**Reconciliation.** When the merchant processor batch does not match the SIS posting, what is the routing.

**Refund processing.** When a student is owed a refund, what is the sequence and the authorization chain.

**Third-party billing.** When an agency or sponsor is the payer, how does the workflow differ.

**Collections.** When an account moves to past due, what are the trigger thresholds and notification cadences.

**Title IV credit balance.** When a Title IV credit produces a refund-due balance, what are the timing requirements and how is compliance shown.

---

## Each failure mode compounds into a different consequence.

Cash application errors compound into reconciliation errors that compound into audit findings. Reconciliation gaps surface in the year-end close and produce deferred revenue adjustments auditors find annually. Refund processing errors produce student complaints that escalate to Title IX, ombudsman, or board correspondence. Third-party billing errors produce sponsor relationship problems. Collections errors produce regulatory exposure. Title IV credit balance errors produce direct compliance findings.

The SOPs that exist for these workflows — when they exist — are usually written at the level of policy rather than workflow. They name the policy but not the routing. They name the regulation but not the exception handling. They name the authority but not the escalation timing.

---

## What closes the gap.

The bursar SOPs that close the gaps are operational. They specify the cash-application matching logic in enough detail that the staff member who absorbs the work in week one can run it without sidebar coaching. They specify the reconciliation cadence and the variance-investigation routing. They specify the refund timing and the authorization chain. Captured from the people who do the work.

---

## A question worth asking now.

What's in your bursar's exception queue right now — and how many of those exceptions repeat every term?

The exception queue is your bursar's measurement instrument. The queue's composition tells your institution what to fix. The SOPs that close the loops are the SOPs your institution should have already had.
