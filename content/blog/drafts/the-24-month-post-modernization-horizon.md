ERP modernization in higher education is typically planned as an eighteen-month project. The plan covers the pre-implementation phase, the configuration and testing phase, the cutover, and the immediate post-go-live stabilization. The plan ends somewhere between three and six months after go-live, when your institution declares the project complete and reassigns the project team.

---

## What rarely gets planned.

The twenty-four months after the project ends. This is the optimization window. It is the period during which your institution discovers what the system does well, where configurations need adjustment, how operational workflows need to evolve, and how the operating model needs to mature to actually realize the value modernization was supposed to produce.

Most institutions do not plan the optimization window. They drift into it.

---

## The drift pattern is predictable.

Your institution operates the new system. Problems surface. Staff build workarounds. Workarounds become permanent. The configuration that was supposed to be optimized is instead worked around. By the end of twenty-four months, your institution has the new system, the workarounds the new system required, and an operating model that did not meaningfully evolve from the pre-modernization state.

---

## What planning the window requires.

The institutions that plan the optimization window have a different experience. They exit twenty-four months post-go-live with the new system, the configurations adjusted to operational reality, and an operating model that has matured.

The structural commitment has three components.

**A named owner with authority.** The optimization window has a named owner with authority to drive configuration changes and operating-model evolution. Typically a senior operations leader, not the project sponsor who has moved on.

**A documented work plan with quarterly milestones.** The plan covers configuration optimization, documentation refresh against the new system, RACI evolution as the new workflows reveal new decision points, and audit-readiness validation under the new system.

**Dedicated documentation capacity.** The institution that defers documentation refresh until after optimization completes never refreshes. The institution that builds the refresh into the optimization plan produces a record that aligns with the new system.

---

## A question worth asking now.

What's your institution's 24-month post-modernization plan — and how much of it depends on documented workflow?

Modernization is the visible work. Optimization is the work that produces the value modernization was supposed to deliver. The institutions that build the discipline build the value.
