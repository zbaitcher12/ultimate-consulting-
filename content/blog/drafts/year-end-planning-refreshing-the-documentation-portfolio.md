The end of the calendar year is the right time for documentation portfolio work. The fall term is closing. The spring term has not started. The audit cycle for the fiscal year that ended in June has reached the field-work and reporting phase. The institutional budget cycle is producing next year's allocations.

The conditions for portfolio-level work are at their best.

---

## What most institutions do instead.

The window is consumed by year-end accounting close, holiday-season planning, and cabinet retreats that produce next year's priorities. The documentation portfolio drifts through another year without the refresh cycle that would keep it load-bearing.

---

## A five-component cadence that works.

The institutions that use the year-end window have a structured cadence.

**Portfolio inventory.** Your institution produces a current-state map. Which documents exist. Which are current under the refresh cadence. Which are stale. Which are missing for workflows that should have documentation.

**Lightweight refresh.** Documents that are current under the cadence but have not been touched in the calendar year receive a lightweight review. Confirms operational alignment, updates the version date, logs the review.

**Full-rewrite identification.** Documents that have drifted materially from operational reality are identified for full rewrite in the next operating cycle.

**Stale-document retirement.** Documents that describe workflows your institution no longer runs are retired from the portfolio. Reduces maintenance load and removes the risk that retired documents are referenced as if current.

**Next-year gap queue.** Workflows that should have documentation and do not are identified. The list becomes next year's development queue.

---

## A question worth asking now.

What's the documentation work your institution should be doing this December — and who owns it?

November and December are the windows for portfolio work. The institutions that use them well preserve the documentation investments of prior years and queue the work next year requires.
