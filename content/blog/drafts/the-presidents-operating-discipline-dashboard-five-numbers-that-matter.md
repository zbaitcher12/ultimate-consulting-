College and university presidents have limited bandwidth for operational metrics. The numbers that reach a president's desk are pre-filtered through the cabinet, the chief of staff, and the strategic planning office. The numbers that survive the filtering are the ones that matter enough to require presidential attention.

Numbers on how the work actually runs rarely survive the filtering. Not because they do not matter, but because they have not been condensed into a form a president can absorb in two minutes.

---

## Five numbers that combine for a decision-useful view.

The combination is more useful than any single number. The trend across the five over four quarters tells a president more than the absolute level at any moment.

**Process coverage.** What percentage of your institution's identified load-bearing workflows have current documentation. Tells the president how much of the operating model is on paper versus carried in memory.

**Refresh currency.** What percentage of the portfolio is current under your institution's refresh cadence. Tells the president whether the portfolio is being kept alive or decaying.

**Governance owner depth.** How many documents are owned by an individual whose departure would create an owner vacancy versus how many are owned through a sustainable governance model. Tells the president how much of the documentation depends on individual staff continuity.

**Audit-finding recurrence.** Of the audit findings produced in the last twenty-four months, how many were raised in prior audits versus how many were new. Tells the president whether your institution learns from audit cycles or repeats the same findings.

**Post-engagement durability.** For documents produced in advisory engagements that closed more than twelve months ago, what percentage are still current and load-bearing versus what percentage have decayed into decoration. Tells the president whether institutional investment in the record produces durable assets or depreciating ones.

---

## Five, not fifteen.

The president cannot absorb fifteen numbers. The president can absorb five. The five chosen are the ones that, in combination, distinguish institutions accumulating operating-model strength from institutions accumulating risk.

---

## A question worth asking now.

Could your institution produce these five numbers for your president this week?

Five numbers, presented well, change how a president allocates attention. The institutions that produce them elevate the record of how the work runs to the strategic conversation. The institutions that don't keep it at the operational level, where strategic protection does not happen.
