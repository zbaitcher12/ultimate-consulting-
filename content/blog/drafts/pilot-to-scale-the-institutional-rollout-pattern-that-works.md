Pilot documentation work typically succeeds. The scope is narrow. The sponsor is engaged. The participants are committed. The document lands.

Your institution exits the pilot with a documented workflow, a named owner, and a refresh cadence.

---

## Scale is where work stalls.

Your institution has one workflow documented and a hundred more that still need attention. The pilot model that worked for one workflow does not obviously translate to ninety-nine more. Your cabinet asks what comes next. The answer is rarely satisfying.

---

## Three rollout patterns that work.

**Function-by-function.** You start with the highest-risk function and move through the remaining functions in priority order. Aid office first. Bursar next. Registrar after. Works when cross-functional handoffs are not the primary risk concentration. Fails when they are.

**Risk-tier-by-risk-tier.** You identify the highest-risk workflows across the entire org chart and address them in priority order, regardless of which function they sit in. Title IV disbursement workflow first. Federal program review evidence workflow next. Works when your institution has clear visibility into where risk concentrates. Fails when functional leadership resists having their function's workflows touched out of sequence.

**Decision-authority-first.** You start with the cross-functional decision-authority chains — the RACI work for the highest-frequency cross-departmental decisions. The within-function workflows follow once the authority chains are clarified. Works when your institution's biggest dysfunction is at the cross-functional handoff. Fails when the within-function workflows are themselves broken.

---

## How the right pattern gets picked.

The institutions that pick the right pattern do it from the operating-model maturity map — the synthesis output from the Q2 functional deep-dives. The map shows where risk concentrates, where handoffs degrade, and where within-function maturity is uneven. The pattern follows the map.

---

## A question worth asking now.

Where on the rollout pattern is your institution right now — and what's the gating decision for moving to the next stage?

Pilot success is the easy part. Scale is where the way your institution operates gets built. The institutions that pick the right rollout pattern for their context are the ones that scale.
