The third quarter of the year, focused on the implementation layer, produces a clarifying observation. The structures that produce internal capability are distinguishable from the structures that produce ongoing dependency. The difference is visible in the first ninety days, not in the finished document.

---

## The distinction is structural, not motivational.

The outside firms in dependency-extending work are not less ethical than the firms in capability-building work. The firms in capability-building work are not more skilled.

The distinction lives in the structure — what is in scope, who owns what, what the finished work includes, and what the handoff looks like.

---

## Five structural markers separate the two patterns.

**Scope includes the transition to internal ownership.** Capability-building work's last finished document is the handoff to the named internal owner. Dependency-extending work's last document is the write-up, with the ownership question deferred.

**Participants include the staff who will own the work afterward.** Capability-building work brings the internal owner in from week one. Dependency-extending work treats the internal owner as a downstream stakeholder.

**Produces governance, not just documents.** Capability-building work specifies the document, the refresh cadence, the change-control workflow, and the version control.

**Transfers methodology, not just outputs.** Capability-building work shows the internal team how to produce the next document without the outside firm. Dependency-extending work protects the methodology as proprietary.

**Ends.** Capability-building work has a defined closure with the internal team taking over. Dependency-extending work transitions to ongoing retainer, additional work, or recurring strategic advisory.

---

## Why the distinction matters in practice.

The distinction matters across every institutional scale, and it shows up most clearly at institutions whose operating budgets are built around institutional capability rather than sustained outside dependence. The institutions that recognize the distinction at scoping commission capability-building work. The institutions that do not commission work that compounds outside cost rather than institutional capability.

---

## A question worth asking now.

Did your most recent outside piece of work leave your institution more capable — or more dependent?

The institutions that exit Q3 with three or four capability-building pieces completed are the institutions building durable operating-model muscle. The institutions that exit with three or four dependency-extending pieces are the institutions paying the same firms for the same work in the next operating cycle.
