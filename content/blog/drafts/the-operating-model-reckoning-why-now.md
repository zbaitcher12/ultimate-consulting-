For most of the last fifteen years, your institution could carry an undocumented operating model and absorb the consequences. Federal regulation was relatively stable. AI deployment was theoretical. Enrollment was uneven but manageable.

That window is closing.

---

## Three pressures. Twenty-four months.

Three pressures are converging. Each one raises the cost of operating in the dark.

**Federal regulation tightening.** Program review cadence is increasing. Title IV documentation expectations are expanding. The institutions that exit reviews cleanly will be the institutions where the way the work runs produces evidence as a byproduct.

**AI deployment readiness.** Most institutions are evaluating AI tools without the record of how the work runs underneath. AI is an accelerator. It accelerates good operating models and bad ones equally. An AI deployment on top of undocumented workflows produces the same inherited-mess pattern ERP go-lives produce, at higher speed.

**The enrollment cliff.** Institutions that lose enrollment have less headcount slack to absorb the carrying cost of undocumented operations. The staff member who knows three load-bearing workflows does not get backfilled. The workflows stop working. The findings accumulate.

---

## Each pressure rewards the same thing.

The pattern across all three is consistent. Each raises the cost of operating-model opacity. Each rewards institutions that have made an explicit, resourced commitment to putting the work on paper. And each penalizes institutions that left the commitment implicit and underfunded.

---

## A question worth asking now.

The window for putting your operating model on paper is the next eighteen months, before the consequences of not doing it become visible.

Has your institution made an explicit, resourced commitment to the record of how the work runs — or is it implicit and underfunded?

The reckoning is not the audit finding or the AI failure or the enrollment shortfall. The reckoning is the decision — made now or deferred — about whether the next operating year is the one in which the institution makes the bet.
